This chart shows the **volatility regime**: how the VIX (CBOE Volatility Index) compares with its own recent average, measured as a rolling Z-score next to the S&P 500 index.

Z-score = (VIX − rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation over the chosen length, using only current and past bars.

:green[***Green shading (Z < −1)***]: VIX is unusually low compared with recent weeks, so the market is calm.
:red[***Red shading (Z > +1)***]: VIX is unusually high, so volatility is elevated. This typically follows a decline that has *already* happened.
